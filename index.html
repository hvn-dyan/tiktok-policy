Official Service Page & Legal Policies

1. About the Application

TikTok Video Publisher is a lightweight desktop client designed to help content creators automate personal video uploads and manage drafts directly to their TikTok accounts via the official TikTok Content Posting API.

  • Compliance: All media published through this application must comply strictly with TikTok Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
  • No Misuse: You agree not to use this software for spamming, distributing malicious files, or conducting unauthorized data collection.
  • Disclaimer: The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind regarding service interruptions or API rate limits imposed by TikTok.

3. Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and handle user data with strict adherence to data protection standards:

  • Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data or credentials on external servers.
  • Token Storage: All OAuth tokens (Access Token and Refresh Token) received from TikTok are encrypted and stored purely on your local machine.
  • Third-Party Sharing: No personal details, account information, or video files are shared with third parties. All network interactions happen directly between your local client and TikTok's official servers.
  • Token Revocation: You can revoke access at any time through your TikTok account settings or by deleting the local configuration file.
